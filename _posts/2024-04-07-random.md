## thoughts and ideas

### research

* dynamic computation seems like a big deal right now, with MOD <d-cite key="raposo2024mixtureofdepths"></d-cite> and Quiet-STaR <d-cite key="zelikman2024quietstar"></d-cite> we can have dynamic routing on tokens with an arbitrary (?) amount of computation spent on each token. sounds like we can craft system that can choose important tokens and reason more on some. combined with some latent representation <d-cite key="lester2024training"></d-cite> we could achieve some sort of abstract reasoning?
* similar idea could be applied to image generation to avoid spending computation on un-important parts of the image and focus on more important, or to filter un-relevant tokens in text conditioning. the current vae used in stable diffusion is a bit of a glorified downscaler, but with a token representation <d-cite key="oord2018neural"></d-cite> the same rationale could be applied.
* gecko embeddings <d-cite key="lee2024gecko"></d-cite> for t2i as it looks they can carry more information than "normal" CLIP and T5 embeddings.
